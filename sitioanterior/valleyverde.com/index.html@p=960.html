Arizona's Drip Irrigation Specialists

We all know that water is scarce in Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tempe and surrounding Arizona communities. Many conclude that an abundance of plants and greenery in our desert landscapes isn’t sustainable due to the scarcity of water source. That is not the case with Landscape Installations provided by Valley Verde Commercial Landscaping We are specialists in employing advanced drip irrigation systems that are very efficient at watering plants while avoiding waste. The average sprinkler irrigation system has a water use efficiency of only around 75%. By contrast, a drip irrigation system performs much better with water use efficiency as high as 90%. This marked difference in water delivery efficiency will make the use of plants on your property viable while also lowering the cost of you water bill.
